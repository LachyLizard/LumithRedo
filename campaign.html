Lumith Campaigns

This part will cover some campaigns that have happened inside Lumith, this will include what was seen in index but also added context.

The Lost Crown of Paralia

Although other campaigns came before this one, this campaign is a good example of what Lumith is, in my personal opinion as a DM; my favourite as of writing this.

This campaign orginally started with a party of 3, throughout the campaign the party grew to about 5-6 players. The party traveled across the islands, and throughout the sky lands in order to resolve their personal issues aswell as save the lost king.

Lost Campaign

Compaired to the others showed off with maps this campaign was done awhile ago and the map has since been lost, the reason why I include this campaign and a reimagined version of the map is because this was the first campaign where I heavily introduced bosses, and a great look into the New Age, or otherwise known as the Era of Runes.

Within this campaign the party of 4 had traveled across to the center island after making a mess of both the town and the party, Fighting both the main civilisation of these islands and great worms in the ground. After witnessing the flagship, a great steel floating monstrosity with a large cannon they made their escape before disapearing into the mountains.

Serpent Side

This campaign helped flesh out the major continents of Lumith aswell as some strong beasts that reside within it.

Serpent side had a great large and everchanging party of misfits that called themselves "Everybody & Nobody" who traveled the province of "Serphia" within one for the three continents; Velari. There they fought, joined, and loss people to various cults and serpents. The party disapeared after a quest gone wrong resulting in a large explosion near the gates of a large city.

In the wise words of my notes; "Serpent flesh goes wild ngl, very tasty.".
