    2. The age of Pre-Shattering was before and over the duration of the great war. It ended after an event dubbed 'The shattering' opened an orb of sorts consuming a great area of one of the three continents.

  2. Post-Shattering

    3. This point covers the end of the great war and onwards untill 'The fog' came to an end.
