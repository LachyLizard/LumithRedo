Lumith Lore

This section will cover the general lore of Lumith and the worldbuilder rather than the campaigns that reside within it.

Lumith Ages

  1. Pre-Shattering

    2. The age of Pre-Shattering was before and over the duration of the great war. It ended after an event dubbed 'The shattering' opened an orb of sorts consuming a great area of one of the three continents.

  2. Post-Shattering

    3. This point covers the end of the great war and onwards untill 'The fog' came to an end.

  3. Era of Runes

    4. This is so far the latest age of Lumith, a society mixing both buildings and people with magic and runes, very similar to the coppper age.

Gods & Lumith Religion

This series of campaigns have two primary pantheons, each godly being is represented by something.

A reminder that this is a spoiler-free list, this list is more or less a what have players encountered or what folktales people know through word of mouth.

    The Astral Pantheon

  • Moon Goddess, not much is known about her although people speak of seeing orchids follow wherever may have been.
  • Lumith Goddess, Its common folklore that she is a sister goddess to the moon, people believe daffodil's are a sign of her.
  • Sun God, Its believed there is one but rarely do people pay much mind, sacarifces and calls to this believed to be god are always unanswered.

    • The Astral Pantheon usually refers to godly beings that do not belong or reside within Lumith.

    The Lumith Pantheon

  • Knowledge Goddess, She is said to grant intellegence to those that intrests her. She is often represented by the people in the shape of owls and those that have had run ins with the being support this claim.
  • Aqua God, Its said that this penguin like being can either be the one being you feel most calm around or the most dangerous, fitting folktales for one believed to be at the helm of the sea.
  • Fog Goddess, Theres not much to say, the Cardinal is a symbol of death, her symbol of death. She is believed to be a friend of the 'Grave Tenders'.
  • Blood God, some who work in medicine have tales of seeing this being, 'a living bloodstream' or 'the sparrow of blood' is how they like to describe the encounters.

    • The Lumith Pantheon usually refers to godly beings that reside within Lumith and come from its lands or its people.
